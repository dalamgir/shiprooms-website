01 — THE VISION

Human expertise.
Agent execution.
Shared understanding.

The factory brings people and AI agents into the same flow of work, with shared goals, knowledge, and accountability across every discipline.

We’re building Shiprooms so people can set direction, contribute their expertise, and review consequential decisions while specialist AI agents plan and execute the work. Requirements, designs, code, reviews, and production insights stay connected as they move between people, agents, and teams.
