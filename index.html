Meet Shiprooms. The AI software factory for the enterprise.

Define

Product teams research opportunities with agents, then define initiatives and measurable outcomes. Agents connect the research and shape a shared plan.

Design

Designers and architects set direction. Agents weigh options and make decisions within agreed boundaries, bringing critical choices to people with recommendations, trade-offs, and impact.

Build

Engineers guide specialist agents building in parallel. They review consequential changes, while agents keep implementation connected to the agreed design.

Verify

Specialist agents check quality and security against enterprise standards. They address routine findings and surface risks that need quality or security teams to investigate.

Ship

Operations and infrastructure teams investigate readiness and approve releases. Agents coordinate deployment, monitor health, and surface issues with the context to act.

Evolve

Agents turn production signals into recommendations. Teams choose what comes next, carrying decisions and learning into the next initiative.

People lead. Agents move work forward. Knowledge compounds. Shiprooms.

01 — THE VISION

Human expertise.
Agent execution.
Shared understanding.

The factory brings people and AI agents into the same flow of work, with shared goals, knowledge, and accountability across every discipline.

We’re building Shiprooms so people can set direction, contribute their expertise, and review consequential decisions while specialist AI agents plan and execute the work. Requirements, designs, code, reviews, and production insights stay connected as they move between people, agents, and teams.
